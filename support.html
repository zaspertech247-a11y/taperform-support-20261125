Make a printable template

Try the built-in Cup collar example, or create a pattern with your own measurements.

Try the Cup collar

  1. In Patterns, tap Load example and then Open pattern. The two diameters are 100 mm and 60 mm, and the axial height is 48 mm.
  2. Developed Shape shows a 52.00 mm slant and a 138.46° sector. Use Labels or Fit, or pinch and drag the drawing.
  3. Use Seam to set the joining edge and tab width. Geometry Check shows developed rim lengths of 188.50 mm and 314.16 mm.
  4. Use Print Layout to choose A4 or US Letter, orientation, pattern rotation, margin and overlap. Freeze report saves the PDF and CSV. Find them again in Reports.
  5. In Report Detail, use Preview PDF, Share PDF or Share CSV. Print at Actual Size or 100 percent. Measure the 100 mm calibration bar before cutting, then align numbered tiles if needed.

Supported geometry

Use concentric circular openings. Equal diameters produce a rectangular wrap. A tapered sleeve requires at least 0.200 mm difference between openings. Diameters and axial height can be 1–2,000 mm. Tabs that reach the opposite edge need a smaller width before freezing a report. Layouts are limited to 64 pages.
