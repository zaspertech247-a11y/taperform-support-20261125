Privacy

Your workspace records

TaperForm does not require an account. It has no advertising or tracking SDK.

Workspace data

The app stores pattern names and measurements, paper settings, frozen reports, and their PDF and CSV files on your device. About → Clear all local records removes the workspace records and any temporary export copies that the app still stores.

Sharing and support

When you share a file using the system share sheet, the destination you choose receives that file. Copies you saved or shared elsewhere remain subject to the recipient or service. When you email our support address, we receive the message and contact details you send to respond to your enquiry.
